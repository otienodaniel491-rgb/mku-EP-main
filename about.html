About Our Institution

Unlocking Infinite Possibilities Through Quality Education

Welcome to Mount Kenya University

We are a premier center of academic excellence dedicated to producing globally competitive graduates. Through a blend of cutting-edge research, robust digital transformation, and holistic student support, we empower the leaders of tomorrow.

To be a center of excellence in education, training, research, and innovation in Africa and beyond.

To provide world-class education, research and innovation for global transformation and sustainable development.

The idea behind the establishment of the University dates back to 1996, when its precursor, the Thika Institute of Technology, was founded as a Computer Outreach Program. At this point in Kenyan history, Information Communication Technology — both mobile telephony and computer technology — had started gaining upward momentum in rural and urban centres.

In 2000, the Institute developed into a commercial college offering management and computer training programmes. Later that year, the Ministry of Education Science and Technology recognized the institute as a full-fledged institution of higher learning and granted it full registration approval.

To remain relevant to the training needs of society, the institute initiated programmes in paramedical, Information Technology, and Business and Entrepreneurship education in 2002.

In 2005, the Institute became the first private institution in Kenya allowed to train Pharmaceutical Technologists by the Pharmacy and Poisons Board of the Ministry of Health.

In 2006, the Commission for Higher Education (CHE) validated and approved the institute's request for collaboration with JKUAT to offer both Diploma and Degree programmes. The Institute continued to work closely with the Commission in line with the stipulated guidelines for establishing a privately funded university.

After fulfilling all requirements stipulated in the guidelines for establishing a privately funded university (1989 rules), the Commission for Higher Education issued MKU with the authority to establish a full-fledged privately funded university, with Thika Institute of Technology as its forerunner.
